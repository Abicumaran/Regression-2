Interpretation of the statistical unit:
The workbook contains no verified donor column. Full sample IDs, not verified
unique donors, are treated as independent units for CIs. If different IDs/levels
share a biological donor, provide the donor mapping before interpreting these
as donor-level confidence intervals. The app's legacy n_donors/Donor Means
labels do not establish independence; analysis_unit and n_independent_units
now explicitly record the grouping used.
